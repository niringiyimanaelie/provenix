# Platform logo sources

These are official brand assets, preserved in their original colors and proportions.
The plugin imports them once as native SPIP article logos. Public templates read
SPIP media rather than these installation files or external URLs.

- Snowflake: SVG wordmark from the official documentation header, https://docs.snowflake.com/ . Brand guidance: https://www.snowflake.com/brand-guidelines/ .
- Databricks: SVG wordmark from the official desktop navigation, https://www.databricks.com/ . Brand guidance: https://brand.databricks.com/ .
- MotherDuck: official media-kit wordmark, https://motherduck.com/images/media-kit/motherduck-wordmark.png .

Retrieved November 5, 2026. Logos belong to their respective owners. Their presence
identifies supported platforms; it does not designate sponsorship or partnership.
Keep the original assets and follow each owner's usage guidance when replacing them.
