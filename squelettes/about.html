ABOUT PROVENIX

Data is complex.
Understanding it shouldn’t be.

We’re building Provenix to make the relationships and logic behind data visible, understandable, and easier to share.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING

Good decisions start
with clear context.

SQL contains a wealth of knowledge: where data originates, how it changes, and what goes into a metric. Too often, that knowledge stays scattered across files and inside people’s heads.

When a new analyst joins, a calculation changes, or a number looks wrong, teams have to reconstruct that story. We believe the code itself should be the starting point for understanding it.

Provenix turns SQL into a connected view of sources, transformations, and outputs. Our focus is practical: help the people working with data see the logic behind it.

WHAT GUIDES US

Clarity, built from the source.

01

Start with the evidence

Extract relationships and formulas from SQL structure. Keep the explanation connected to the code behind it.

02

Make complexity readable

Bring detailed technical context into a visual experience that supports investigation and conversation.

03

Build with real questions

Focus on the everyday work of understanding pipelines, reviewing calculations, and onboarding to unfamiliar data.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

A focused product.
A bigger ambition.

Provenix is in active development. Today, our work centers on SQL analysis, column-level lineage, formula inspection, and visual exploration.

Our longer-term ambition is to connect that technical foundation to richer business context and documentation. We’re building toward that vision thoughtfully, and welcome conversations with teams facing these challenges.
